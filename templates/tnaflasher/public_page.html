Your browser will ask you to pick the 29F1:0230 device twice: once when you click Flash, and again a few seconds later after the miner switches into flashing mode and reconnects. Both are expected — just select the same device each time and the flash carries on. It's not an error.

  1. Power off the Avalon.
  2. Hold the small recovery / BOOT button next to the USB-C port.
  3. While holding it, plug in the double-A USB cable (USB-A both ends) to your PC.
  4. Power on, then release the button after ~2 seconds.
  5. The miner now shows up as a USB device 29F1:0230.
